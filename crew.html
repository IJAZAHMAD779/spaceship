Solar Voyage FAQs

How close does the Astral Cruiser 5000 get to the Sun? +
During maximum perihelion orbital approach, the cruiser flies within 0.038 AU (approx. 5.68 million kilometers) of the solar photosphere—closer than Mercury and inside the corona.
Is radiation dangerous for passengers and scientists onboard? +
No. The ship employs a dual-tier protection system: an active 14.2 Tesla toroidal magnetic deflector field combined with a multilayer gold-aerogel thermal shield that reduces interior radiation to below Earth-sea-level standards.
Do passengers experience weightlessness or artificial gravity? +
The main habitation quarters, dining lounges, and staterooms are situated in the centrifugal rotating ring providing 1.0 Earth-G. Microgravity science pods are accessible on the central hub.
What pre-flight training is required for guests? +
All voyagers undergo a 5-day acclimatization protocol at Gateway Spaceport 13 in Low Earth Orbit covering emergency pressure gear, space navigation basics, and vestibular orientation.
